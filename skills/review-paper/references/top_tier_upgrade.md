# Top-Tier Upgrade

Load this file when the paper has real potential and the user wants advice that raises the paper's ceiling rather than just fixing defects.

## Core Principle

Top-tier papers do not merely avoid mistakes. They make a sharp contribution, earn causal credibility, explain why the result matters, and present the evidence in a way that makes the paper feel inevitable rather than incremental.

## Upgrade Framework

Use these five questions:

1. What is the strongest one-sentence contribution claim the evidence can honestly support?
2. What is the single highest-return credibility upgrade?
3. What is missing that would make the paper feel important rather than just careful?
4. What should be cut, reframed, or reorganized so the argument becomes sharper?
5. Which improvements are feasible in revision, and which would require a different paper?

## High-Return Upgrade Levers

### 1. Sharpen Contribution

- Replace broad claims with a precise contribution relative to the nearest 3 to 5 papers.
- State not only what is different about the setting, but what is learned because of that difference.
- If the paper is incremental, identify the narrowest honest claim that still feels useful.

### 2. Increase Credibility

- Add the one test that would most move a skeptical referee.
- Surface the hardest threat instead of burying it.
- Use institutional detail to tighten the exogeneity story.
- Replace generic robustness tables with design-specific falsification or sensitivity analysis.

### 3. Raise Importance

- Translate estimates into policy, welfare, or theory-relevant quantities.
- Show how the result changes what a reader should believe, not just what the coefficient sign is.
- Add the most informative heterogeneity or mechanism result, but only if it is framework-driven and interpretable.

### 4. Tighten Theory-Empirics Fit

- Ensure the framework generates the comparisons the empirics actually estimate.
- Cut decorative theory that does not discipline the evidence.
- If no formal model is needed, articulate a clean conceptual framework anyway.

### 5. Improve Narrative And Presentation

- Make the introduction state the question, design, main result, and contribution early.
- Cut paragraphs, tables, and side analyses that do not earn their place.
- Replace cluttered tables with clearer progression and stronger figures.
- Make the paper easy to teach from.

## Feasible Revision Advice Versus Different-Paper Advice

### Feasible in revision

- Reframe the contribution more honestly and sharply.
- Add one or two design-specific tests.
- Clarify sample construction and estimand.
- Improve figures, table progression, and exposition.
- Add better magnitude interpretation or policy translation.

### Usually requires a different paper

- A new source of identification.
- A fundamentally different dataset.
- A mechanism section that depends on uncollected outcomes.
- A contribution claim that the current evidence cannot support even with perfect exposition.

## How To Write Upgrade Advice

- Give 3 to 6 suggestions, ordered by expected payoff.
- Separate "must fix for credibility" from "would raise ceiling."
- Be concrete: name the test, comparison, reframing, or cut.
- Avoid fantasy advice that ignores the actual design or data.
- If the paper is unlikely to reach top-tier level, say so plainly and redirect the advice toward the best realistic target.

## Useful Upgrade Prompts

- What would make a skeptical top-journal referee revise upward by one full notch?
- What is the one figure, table, or test the paper is currently missing?
- What claim should be narrowed so the rest of the paper becomes more convincing?
- Which section should be cut or merged to make the paper feel sharper?
- What additional evidence would transform the paper from competent to memorable?
